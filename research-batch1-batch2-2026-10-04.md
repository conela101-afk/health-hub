## Corrections to earlier Batch 1 claims
- 65+ flu vaccine is adjuvanted (Fluad), not "high-dose". All 65+; 60-64 and at-risk adults get standard vaccine.
- HSE PPV23 page is overdue for review (20/09/2026) and predates pharmacy circular NCO-15-2026 (healthy 65+ with GMS/GPVC/HAA card via pharmacy; at-risk go to GP).
- NI Universal Credit: automatic passporting for Help with Health Costs from 1 Dec 2025 (take-home pay £435 or less, or £935 or less with child element / limited capability for work). Any "UC does not qualify" wording is outdated.
- BreastCheck: 50-69, about every 2 years, sometimes 3. **Applied.**
- 8 Oct flu launch date is secondary only; official wording is "beginning of October 2026".
- ROI shingles (Shingrix) remains unfunded; a non-official clinic site wrongly claims HSE funding for 65+.
