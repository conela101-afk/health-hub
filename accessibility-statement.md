## Our commitment

Health Hub is built to be usable by anyone navigating health services in Ireland and Northern Ireland, including people using assistive technology, people with low vision, and people navigating the site while distressed or in a hurry. We're a small, volunteer-run project and this statement reflects where we are, not a finished state.

## What we've done

- Skip-to-content link on every page (confirmed present in `index.html` / `styles.css`, 6 Sep 2026)
- Calm mode: reduces visual intensity for users who find the default styling overstimulating (confirmed present in `app.js`, 6 Sep 2026)
- Large touch targets and generous spacing throughout
- Plain-language content, avoiding clinical jargon where possible

## What we haven't fully tested yet

- Full keyboard-only navigation across all pages
- Screen reader compatibility (VoiceOver, NVDA, TalkBack)
- WCAG 2.1 AA colour contrast verification outside of Calm mode

We're working through these and will update this statement as each is verified. See `coverage-gap-tracker.md` §5 for the standing checklist this statement is gated on.
