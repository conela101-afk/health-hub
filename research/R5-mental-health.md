**Draft:** Ireland: If you are detained under the Mental Health Act 2001, your detention is automatically reviewed by an independent Mental Health Tribunal. You are given a free legal representative, an independent psychiatrist reviews your care, and you can attend and speak at the tribunal. A new Mental Health Act 2026 has been signed (link only, not yet commenced, checked 27 Oct 2026). Northern Ireland: RQIA has a legal duty to look into possible ill-treatment, poor care or improper detention of people under the Mental Health Order.
