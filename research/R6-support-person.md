**Unclear:** bringing someone is framed as "ask if you can" on the HSE page, not a right; National Consent Policy wording on support persons quoted from a non-HSE mirror of the 2022 text, confirm in the current HSE PDF.

**Draft:** You can ask the clinic in advance whether you can bring someone with you. During the appointment, it can help to write down what is said, check that you understand the next steps in your care, and ask who to contact if you have more questions. If you cannot raise a concern yourself, a relative, carer or advocate can do it for you. If you are at a Mental Health Tribunal, you can ask your legal representative about bringing a support person.

**Gaps:** **no official page found saying a patient may ask for a copy of a letter or care plan, or for written confirmation of what was agreed.** Do not include either in any checklist until sourced. Follow-up: HSE data protection (subject access request) and Freedom of Information pages. No NI support-person source.
