# "Nobody replied?" fact checklist, 26 Nov 2026

A read-only inventory of the facts the "Nobody replied?" feature would rely on, for the maintainer to open and check. It adds no new facts or URLs: everything below is read from `data.js` and `tools.js` as they are on `main`. The gate (see `MASTER-BUILD-PLAN.md`, Decisions 26 Nov 2026) is that every statutory timeframe, escalation step and next-body route the feature uses has been checked on an official page a person opened.

## Summary (the maintainer's to-do list for the gate)

- Facts in scope: **59** distinct `TOOL_FACTS` (some are used by more than one route).
- **Opened by a person:** 16. These are facts whose `last_verified` is 19 Nov 2026 (cleared by the maintainer in #147) or 9 Oct 2026 (verification sprint 1, pages opened in a browser).
- **Still `verify: true`:** 6.
- **Flag clear, but not shown as opened:** 37. Most are dated 28 Sep 2026, which the `data.js` header says was a search-result check, "weaker than reading the page". Others carry only a date. These count as unchecked for the gate until a person opens the page.
- **Gaps** (a step the feature would need that has no fact in the repo): **22**, listed under each of the five sections.
- **Timeframes stated in letter templates with no source URL or flag:** 7 templates, listed in the last section. These also need checking, because a chaser letter would repeat them.

How to read the table:
- **verify status** is the field in the data. The text after it says how it was checked, from `last_verified`, the `data.js` header and the verification-sprint entries in the changelog archive.
- **used for** is derived by rule, so adjust it if the feature is scoped differently: *overdue check* = the fact carries a period (`calc`) used for dates, or is a navigator deadline, tracker or AON calculator input; *next step* = it appears in a navigator stage, the AON ladder or the tracker; *chaser letter* = a letter would quote its timeframe.
- A `(period used for dates)` note is the number the tools do arithmetic with. Check it against the page, not only the wording.
