**Draft:** Professional regulators look at whether an individual doctor, nurse, midwife, pharmacist, dentist or health and social care professional is fit to practise. They are not complaint services for hospitals or clinics, and they cannot arrange compensation or make someone apologise. In Ireland: Medical Council (doctors), NMBI (nurses and midwives), CORU (health and social care professionals), PSI (pharmacists), Dental Council (dentists). In Northern Ireland, UK regulators such as the GMC and GDC cover doctors and dentists. For a problem with a service, use the service's own complaints process.

**Gaps:** NMC (NI) not researched and nmc.org.uk is not yet approved; NI-specific GMC page; PSNI (pending approval); current Medical Council web complaints page.
