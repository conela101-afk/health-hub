**Draft (under 120 words):** If something goes wrong in your care, or harm may have happened, the HSE says it will talk to you openly and honestly about what happened and your ongoing care. This is called open disclosure. For certain very serious incidents listed in law, open disclosure is mandatory and includes a meeting and a written follow-up. You can ask the service that provided your care about open disclosure. If you are not satisfied, you can use the HSE complaints process. The Patient Advocacy Service offers free, independent support after a patient safety incident in a public hospital or nursing home.
