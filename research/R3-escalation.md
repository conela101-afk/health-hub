**Escalation order (as described by official pages).** ROI: local resolution, then formal complaint (Your Service Your Say), then internal review, then Ombudsman (or Ombudsman for Children for under-18s). HIQA is a place to share concerns, not an individual complaint route. NI: provider's complaints procedure (two-stage NIPSO model in Trusts), then NIPSO. RQIA does not investigate individual complaints.

**Unclear:** HSE 12-month limit from Citizens Information only; Ombudsman for Children pages not opened; HIQA's own "Report a Concern or Give Feedback" wording ("While we do not have the legal power to investigate individual complaints, we do review all information or concerns about services we receive and we assess them against the regulations and the standards") taken from search-result text; open the page to confirm; NI time limit worded differently on nidirect and Trust pages (quote nidirect).

**Draft:** Ireland: Start with the service. If you are not satisfied, make a formal complaint through the HSE's Your Service Your Say process and ask for a review. After that, you can go to the Office of the Ombudsman, or the Ombudsman for Children for under-18s. The Ombudsman asks that complaints are made within 12 months, but may accept later ones for good reason. HIQA does not investigate individual complaints, but you can tell it about concerns. Northern Ireland: Use the HSC organisation's complaints procedure first. If you are still unhappy, you can go to the NI Public Services Ombudsman.

**Gaps:** HSE www2.hse.ie complaints page and time limit (primary); Ombudsman for Children health complaints page; HIQA page full read.

## Time limits (copied from official pages; show in the app only after Elaine opens the page, with the URL)
ROI: Ombudsman within 12 months of the action or decision (later accepted for good reason; use the provider's process first) (https://ombudsman.ie/en/collection/72275-your-questions/). Ombudsman, public hospital: within 12 months of the date of the Complaints Officer's or HSE Review Officer's report (https://www.ombudsman.ie/publications/information-leaflets/the-ombudsman-and-the-hea/). HSE Your Service Your Say: within 12 months of the event or awareness (Citizens Information only; open the HSE page) (https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/health/health-system/making-a-complaint-about-the-health-service-executive/).
NI: nidirect: usually within 6 months of awareness, normally no later than 12 months after the event (https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/how-complain-or-raise-concerns-about-health-services). NIPSO: usually not accepted more than 6 months after the provider process ends (https://www.nipso.org.uk/faqs).
No limit found: Ombudsman for Children.
